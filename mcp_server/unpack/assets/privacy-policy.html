Unpack – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Version: 1.1

Summary: Unpack is a stateless brainstorming tool. We do not store or retain any personal data. Your input is processed transiently within your ChatGPT session. Any conversation history is managed by OpenAI according to their policies.

2. What Unpack Does

Unpack is a facilitated brainstorming tool that helps you:

  • Formulate a clear problem statement
  • Generate questions about your problem
  • See your questions clustered by theme

Unpack runs entirely within ChatGPT. It does not have its own backend database or user accounts.

3. Data Controller

Unpack does not act as a data controller for stored personal data, as it does not store or retain any personal data.

OpenAI acts as the data controller for ChatGPT interactions. Please refer to OpenAI's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle your data.

4. Data We Process

When you use Unpack, you may enter free-text input such as problem descriptions and questions. This input may contain personal data.

How we handle this data:

  • Your input is processed transiently to generate structured output (clusters, themes)
  • Processing occurs within your ChatGPT session only
  • Unpack does not store or retain any of this data
  • We do not create user accounts or profiles
  • We do not use cookies or tracking technologies

5. Lawful Basis for Processing

Any transient processing of input data is performed solely to provide the requested brainstorming functionality. The lawful basis under GDPR is legitimate interest in delivering the service you have requested.

6. Third-Party Processing

Unpack operates within OpenAI's ChatGPT platform. Your interactions are subject to:

  • OpenAI's Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI's Terms of Use

OpenAI may process and store conversation data according to their own policies, including for safety monitoring and service improvement. Unpack has no control over OpenAI's data practices.

7. Data Retention

Unpack does not retain any data. Once your session ends, no data persists on Unpack's side.

Your ChatGPT conversation history is managed by OpenAI according to their retention policies. If you have conversation history enabled in ChatGPT, your interactions may be stored by OpenAI.

8. Your Rights

Since Unpack does not store personal data, requests such as data access, correction, or deletion cannot be fulfilled by Unpack directly — there is no data for us to access, correct, or delete.

For data processed and stored by ChatGPT/OpenAI, please refer to OpenAI's Privacy Policy to exercise your rights under applicable data protection laws.

9. Children's Privacy

Unpack is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly process information from children. Use of ChatGPT is subject to OpenAI's age requirements.

10. Security

All communication between your browser and ChatGPT is encrypted via HTTPS. Since Unpack does not store data, there is no data at rest to secure on our end.

11. International Users

Unpack is available globally through ChatGPT. Data processing occurs on OpenAI's infrastructure, which may be located outside your country of residence. Please refer to OpenAI's policies for details on international data transfers.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Unpack's functionality changes. Updates will be reflected in the "Effective Date" and "Version" at the top of this page.
