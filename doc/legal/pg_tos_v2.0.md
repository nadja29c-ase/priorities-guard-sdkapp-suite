Addendum A: Priority Guardian
This addendum supplements the general Terms above with provisions specific to Priority Guardian.

A.1 Service Description
Priority Guardian helps you track time spent on your top priorities by connecting to your Google Calendar (read-only). You declare up to 3 active priorities, each assigned a calendar keyword (P1, P2, or P3). Priority Guardian matches your calendar events against these keywords and computes weekly time allocations.

A.2 Google Calendar Access
Priority Guardian uses the Google Calendar API scope calendar.events.readonly. It reads event titles, descriptions, start/end times, event status, and your personal response status (accepted, declined, tentative). It explicitly restricts the API response to only these fields — Google never sends us event locations, organizer emails, attendee lists, attachments, or video call links.

Priority Guardian never writes to your calendar. It does not create, modify, or delete events. If a future version requires write access, we will request separate consent.

A.3 What Is Stored vs. Computed
Stored: Priority names, types, keywords, deadlines, targets, checkpoint dates, goals, deliverables, weekly aggregated hours, streak counts, and running averages. All user-authored text. Google OAuth tokens (AES-256 encrypted).

Computed and discarded: Event titles and descriptions are processed in real-time to compute hours and semantic groupings, then discarded. Only the aggregated numbers persist. For the current week's report, event titles and descriptions are passed to ChatGPT for semantic grouping of non-priority time — this data is not stored by our backend.

A.4 Data Accuracy Disclaimer
Time computations depend on keyword matching against your calendar event titles. Events without the correct keyword are not counted. Events with incorrect keywords are counted toward the wrong priority. Time zones, DST transitions, recurring event exceptions, and cancelled events may affect accuracy. We make reasonable efforts to handle these edge cases but do not guarantee perfect accuracy.

A.5 Deletion Behavior
Deleting a priority permanently deletes all associated goals, deliverables, and weekly snapshots via database cascade. This action cannot be undone. Retiring a priority freezes it (read-only) without deleting data — retired priorities can still be viewed but are no longer updated.



Addendum B: MindSort AI
This addendum supplements the general Terms above with provisions specific to MindSort AI.

B.1 Service Description
MindSort AI helps you visually structure unstructured thoughts by organizing them into themed clusters, subclusters, and priorities. It does not connect to Google Calendar or any external data source. All input comes directly from you through ChatGPT.

B.2 Data Model
MindSort AI stores one board per user. Every time you organize your thoughts, your board is automatically saved to the database and overwrites the previous version. There is no version history and no undo. Board data consists of cluster names, subcluster names, thought items, behavioral tags, and priority assignments (up to 3, with rank P1/P2/P3 and optional reason: "blocker" or "goal-aligned") — all user-authored content structured by the language model.

B.3 No External Connections
MindSort AI does not require Google Calendar or any OAuth connection beyond the standard ChatGPT authentication. It processes only the text you provide during the conversation.

B.4 What Gets Sent to OpenAI
Your full board content — cluster names, subcluster names, thought items, tags, and priority assignments (including rank and optional reason) — is included in requests and responses exchanged with the ChatGPT runtime so the language model can display, organize, and update your board conversationally. When you use the thought-organizing and prioritization features, the text you provide to confirm you are done adding thoughts, state your prioritization criterion, or name your priorities is also included. We do not intentionally include direct account identifiers such as your name or email address in these transfers.

B.5 Data Retention
Board content (clusters, thought items, tags, priorities), content hash, and board snapshot timestamp are retained until you delete your board in-app or request full account deletion. For shared retention periods (OAuth tokens, browser session, consent records, audit logs, database backups), see Section 6 and the Privacy Policy.

B.6 Deletion Behavior
You can delete your MindSort AI board at any time by asking ChatGPT to delete your data. The app will confirm exactly what will be deleted before proceeding: your saved board with all its clusters, thought items, tags, and priorities. Once confirmed, all board data is permanently deleted. This action cannot be undone. Please note: a deletion audit log entry is retained separately for up to 90 days for compliance purposes, after which it is permanently deleted. For full account deletion, contact support@priority-guard.com.

B.7 AI Clustering Disclaimer
The clustering, subclustering, and tagging of your thoughts is performed by ChatGPT's language model. Results are non-deterministic and may vary between sessions. Clusters are organizational suggestions — not assessments, diagnoses, or recommendations. You decide what to do with the structured output.
